# Returns policy

## Refunds

Refunds are issued within 14 days of the day we receive your returned item. The money goes back to the
original payment method. Bank processing can add a few extra days on top of that.

## Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery. Exchanges are free
of charge when the item is unworn and still has its tags attached.

## Damaged items

If your order arrives damaged, contact support with a photo of the damage and your order number. We will
send a replacement or refund you in full, and you do not need to return the damaged item.
